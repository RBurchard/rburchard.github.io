Researcher & Ph.D. Candidate

Robin Burchard

Ubiquitous Computing Group (Prof. Dr. Kristof Van Laerhoven) · Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science · University of Siegen

I am a researcher and Ph.D. student in the Ubiquitous Computing Group at the University of Siegen. My research focuses on Wearable Computing and Human Activity Recognition (HAR) in unconstrained real-world environments. In particular, I investigate how multi-modal sensor fusion—combining inertial wrist motion (IMUs), atmospheric environmental sensors (barometric pressure, humidity, temperature), and audio—can resolve ambiguities in complex Activities of Daily Living and health sensing applications such as everyday and compulsive handwashing detection.

Featured Dataset & Benchmark 2026

HARMES: A Multi-Modal Dataset for Wearable Human Activity Recognition with Motion, Environmental Sensing and Sound

Robin Burchard, Pascal-André Brückner, Marius Bock, Jürgen Gall, Kristof Van Laerhoven

arXiv Preprint

A multi-modal wearable HAR dataset combining wrist-worn IMUs, atmospheric environmental sensors (humidity, temperature, pressure), and audio — featuring over 80 hours of recordings from 20 participants across 15 Activities of Daily Living.
